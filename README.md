# Santiago Valenzuela

I am a Data Scientist and Computer Engineering student at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile, focused on machine learning systems that make it into production.

Over the last three years I have worked on AI projects for the financial and mining sectors: building LLM-based applications for document processing, deploying predictive models, and designing the data pipelines that support them. In 2025 my team won the Global66 Global Challenge, selected among more than 500 students, with an AI-driven financial health system for expense categorization and behavior prediction.

## What I work on

**Machine Learning engineering.** From feature engineering and model training (Scikit-learn, Keras) to deployment and monitoring. I am particularly interested in time series forecasting and the MLOps practices needed to keep models reliable over time.

**LLM applications.** Retrieval-augmented generation, document processing pipelines, and chatbots built with LangChain, deployed on IBM Cloud, AWS, and GCP.

**Backend and data infrastructure.** Django and Flask services, ETL processes, and database design across PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and S3.
